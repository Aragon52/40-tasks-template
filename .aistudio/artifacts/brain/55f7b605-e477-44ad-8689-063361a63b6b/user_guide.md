# User Guide: Walmart Order Tasking & Platform Features

Welcome to the Walmart Premium Order Matching and Earning Platform. This guide outlines how to use the key features to manage your retail matching tasks efficiently.

## 1. Order Tasking (Matching Page)

The "Order Pickup" (Matching) page is the heart of your daily activities. It connects your account with retail allocation worksheets.

### How it Works:
1. **Accessing the Page**: Navigate to the "Picking" tab (the central, highlighted button in the bottom navigation).
2. **Initiating Tasks**:
   - Ensure your account balance is positive and sufficient for the required order tier.
   - Click the "Begin Picking" button.
   - The system initiates a real-time matching algorithm. You will see a visual reel spinning to match your account with an available retail order.
3. **Task Completion**:
   - Once matched, an order detail modal will appear.
   - Review the order details and submit to lock in the commission.
   - Commissions are credited to your balance immediately upon successful submission.

### Important Rules:
- **Liquidity**: You must maintain a positive balance to match and accept retail allocation worksheets.
- **Commitment**: Jobs cannot be canceled midway. Canceling before completion will result in system restrictions, including the inability to withdraw.
- **Limits**: Daily task limits apply based on your current VIP tier.

---

## 2. Assignment Methods & VIP Tiers

Assignments are determined by your account tier, balance, and recruitment history.

### The Assignment Algorithm:
- **Retail Node Distribution**: The system uses an automated node distribution algorithm that pairs users with order worksheets.
- **VIP Tiers**: Your VIP tier determines your commission rate and the number of matching tasks you can perform per day. Higher tiers generally allow for higher transaction volumes.
- **Staff Nodes**: New accounts are bound to a specific staff node via a referral code. Your assignment efficiency may be influenced by your node’s activity levels.

---

## 3. Platform Features

### Order Management
- **Dashboard**: View your total earnings, commissions, and balance at a glance.
- **Order History**: Track your past orders in the "Orders" tab. Orders are categorized as:
  - **Success**: Commission processed.
  - **Frozen**: Order is held for verification.

### Financial Management
- **Deposits**: Use the "Deposit" page to top up your balance to ensure you meet the liquidity requirements for order matching.
- **Withdrawals**: Once you have met your commission targets, you can request a withdrawal via the "Withdraw" page.

### Affiliate & Team Management
- **Referrals**: Grow your earnings by inviting new merchants using your unique invitation code.
- **Team Ledger**: Monitor your team's performance and associated commission distributions in the "Affiliate" section.

### Support
- **Live Chat**: If you encounter issues with matching, deposits, or order fulfillment, use the "Support" tab for instant, real-time assistance.

---

## 4. Troubleshooting
- **Cannot Start Task**: Ensure you have sufficient balance and have not reached your daily task limit.
- **Order Frozen**: Ensure all account information is correct. Check the "Orders" page for details.
- **Withdrawal Denied**: Verify that all active tasks have been completed and your account is not under restriction for canceling tasks midway.
